# Recommendations & Recently Viewed Specification

1. **Purpose**: Surface relevant jewellery pieces based on shared category/material/collections and track recently viewed products.
2. **Components**: `RecentlyViewedSection`, `ProductDetailView` (`YOU MAY ALSO LIKE`), and `CartDrawer` (`You May Also Like`).
3. **Storage**: Recently viewed product IDs are persisted in `localStorage` (`jco_demo_recent_v1`, max 8 items).
