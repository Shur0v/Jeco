# Navigation & Mega Menu Specification

1. **Purpose**: Provide site-wide discovery across categories, collections, and precious materials on desktop and mobile.
2. **Components**: `AnnouncementBar`, `Header`, `MegaMenu`, `MobileNavDrawer`.
3. **Mega Menu Variants**:
   - **Shop Mega Menu**: `DISCOVER`, `BY CATEGORY` (2 sub-columns), `BY MATERIAL`, plus `DEMI-FINE` and `LUXURY` editorial feature cards.
   - **14K Gold Mega Menu**: `BY CATEGORY`, `BY COLLECTION`, `BY MATERIAL`.
4. **Keyboard & Focus**: Closes automatically on `Escape` key press or mouse leave; full mobile drawer for touch viewports.
