# Responsive Breakpoint Standards

- **320px – 639px (Mobile)**: 2-column product grids, slide-out `MobileNavDrawer`, stacked PDP gallery and purchase module, full-width `CartDrawer`.
- **640px – 1023px (Tablet)**: 3-column product grids, adaptive forms and footer columns.
- **1024px – 1920px+ (Desktop)**: `1440px` max-width container, 4-column catalog grids, hover/focus `MegaMenu`, 2-column 7-tile PDP gallery with sticky right purchase module.
